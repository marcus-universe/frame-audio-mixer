# Icons

The Frame Audio Mixer logo is in `assets/logo/` as SVG, in blue and pink. `assets/icons/` holds the PNGs that the panel and the dashboard use. Record, Stop and Play are drawn shapes. The other panel glyphs are embedded paths, not fetched at runtime:
